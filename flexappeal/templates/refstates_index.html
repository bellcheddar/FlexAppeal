Reference states

Two systems simulated from known start states, with every motif scored against the experimental structures of the same protein. The kinase motifs are resolved through KLIFS and the receptor's through GPCRdb, so each residue is addressed by the number the catalogue gives it rather than one written here.

How this works, and every citation behind it.

Each motif carries two readings side by side: how close it sits to the nearest active and nearest inactive reference, and what the published rule-based criterion says. Where they disagree the motif is marked conflict rather than averaged, because a disagreement between geometry and rule is the interesting case, not noise to be removed.
