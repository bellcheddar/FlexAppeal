Every mixed-case figlet font hangs the p's descender off the letter's stem rather than its bowl, because that is where a descender belongs typographically. At this resolution it misreads badly. As pyfiglet emits it, the first leg sits at columns 9-11 while its bowl sits at 11-14, and the second bowl is at 17-20 -- so each leg falls in the gap BETWEEN the two bowls and the eye pairs it with the one on its right. The legs look shifted right, and no font fixes it: it is how they are all drawn. So the two descender rows are shifted right by two columns, which puts each leg directly under its own bowl and removes the ambiguity.
