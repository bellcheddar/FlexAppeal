How these were chosen

Kinase references come from KLIFS, filtered to human structures at 2.8 Å or better with a pocket quality score of at least 6 and no more than five missing pocket residues. Receptor references come from GPCRdb at 3.6 Å or better, split by the catalogue's own state assignment. Nothing was hand-picked.
