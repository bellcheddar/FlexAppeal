What the state score means

For each motif, s compares how far it sits from the nearest active reference against how far from the nearest inactive one, as (dinactive − dactive) / (dinactive + dactive). It runs from +1, sitting on the active references, to −1 on the inactive ones. A normalised difference rather than a raw gap, so a three-residue motif and a twenty-residue loop land on the same axis instead of the larger one dominating.

Both distances are measured after superposing on the structural core, so what is reported is the motif moving rather than the whole protein drifting. conflict means the geometry and the published rule-based criterion disagree, which is shown rather than resolved.

State score over time

How to read this

One line per motif. Above +0.2 the geometry is calling the motif active-like and below −0.2 inactive-like; between them it is intermediate, and the bands are drawn to show that. Raw per-frame values, not smoothed.

Secondary structure over time

How to read this

One column per saved frame, one row per residue, coloured by the DSSP class. A motif unwinding shows up as a horizontal band changing colour part-way across. Computed with the full eight-class DSSP rather than the simplified three, because the difference between an α helix and a 3-10 helix is the kind of local change worth seeing.

A residue DSSP could not assign is left blank rather than drawn as coil: no assignment and an assignment of coil are different claims, and only one of them is evidence of anything.

Reference state map

What this plane is

Every reference structure plotted by the geometry its state is read from, coloured and shaped by the catalogue's own label. For a kinase this is the Dunbrack plane: the DFG phenylalanine ring measured against the αC-Glu+4 Cα and against the catalytic lysine Cα.

The dashed line is the published 11.0 Å threshold. It is drawn because of where it falls: the two classes separate by 8.5 Å on the difference between the two distances, and the line passes through the corner of the DFG-out cluster, which is why six of ABL1's own DFG-out references were being called neither state. Reading the state from which anchor the ring is nearer needs no threshold where the data is dense, and takes agreement with KLIFS from 11 of 17 to 17 of 17.

What these should be

POPC at 303 K is expected to give 60 to 68 Å² per lipid and a phosphate-to-phosphate thickness of 36 to 41 Å. Area per lipid is the box cross-section divided by the lipids in one leaflet, with the leaflets split at their own midplane rather than at z = 0, because the bilayer drifts in z under a membrane barostat.

Why the core matters

Every motif distance here is measured after superposing on this core, so the core is the frame of reference the whole score is expressed in. It is chosen to be the part of the fold that does not move on activation; if it did move, the motif distances would report the core's motion instead.
